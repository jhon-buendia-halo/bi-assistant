Scope

  • a second Context card User personas after Knowledge (app-shell R3a), opening a User personas section: a list of personas sorted by name with an empty state, a New persona button, and an editor to create, edit and delete one. A persona has name (required, unique ignoring case, at most 80 characters), role (at most 80), description (at most 2,000), goals (at most 2,000), typicalQuestions (0 to 10, each at most 300), expertise (novice, intermediate default, expert) and answerStyle (at most 500). Backend module personas with collection user_personas and GET/POST /personas, PATCH/DELETE /personas/:id. New capability spec user-personas
  • api.md, data-model.md, ui.md and app-shell R3a updated.

Out of scope: agents or sessions using personas; picking a persona for a session; importing or generating personas; evals.

Acceptance

  • the Context page shows Knowledge then User personas
  • the card opens the section under the Context back control
  • a persona can be created with every field, edited and deleted, and survives a restart
  • a duplicate name or a blank name is refused with its message
  • backend and frontend unit tests, builds, check-specs and the navigation Playwright spec green.
