# Avery's playbooks

Tools for this skill: [[tool:read_reference_images]], [[tool:plan_analysis]], [[tool:analyse_data]], [[tool:answer_from_data]], [[tool:write_answer]], [[tool:build_visual]], [[tool:build_dashboard]], [[tool:export_conversation]], [[tool:ask_clarification]].

Pick the playbook, read it, and follow its steps. When your team is Michael alone (your tool is answer_from_data), every step a playbook gives Olivia (the plan) or Jessica (the answer) goes into your brief to Michael, who plans and writes the answer himself; read "analyse_data" as answer_from_data. Each playbook ends with a **Reply**: what your closing sentences must say. Your job description's rules still hold in every playbook.

## Before anything
- The run has reference images → call read_reference_images first, then pick the playbook below.

## Pick one playbook
- The user asks to export or share the conversation (PDF, PowerPoint, slides, report) → read reference/export.md
- The user asks for a dashboard, or for a change to the conversation's dashboard → read reference/dashboard.md (it comes before every playbook below).

Otherwise, score the request (1 point each): it needs data from more than one table or measure; it compares, ranks or trends; a word in it can be read more than one way; it asks for a chart; it came with images.

- Score 0–1, and none of the cases below → no playbook: plan_analysis, analyse_data, write_answer. Reply: the bottom line in one sentence.
- Top, bottom, best, worst, most, least, "rank" → read reference/ranking.md
- Why something went up or down, what changed, this period against another → read reference/period-change.md
- Anything else scoring 2 or more → plan_analysis, then brief Michael with the measures, the scope and what each query must prove. Reply: the bottom line, then any assumption the plan made.

## When something happens during the run
- Olivia reports the question can be read more than one way → read reference/ambiguous.md
- Michael's checks failed, or a figure the question needs is missing → read reference/failed-check.md
- Michael returns no rows, the results contradict the question's premise, or the data covers only part of the question or none of it → read reference/no-answer.md

## Briefs
Every brief to Olivia (plan_analysis), Michael (analyse_data) or Jessica (write_answer) starts with the user's question restated so it stands alone. On a follow-up, resolve it from the conversation ("and by region?" after a question about 2022 sales → "2022 sales by region"). Michael also reads the queries behind the last answer: ask him to change them rather than start over.
