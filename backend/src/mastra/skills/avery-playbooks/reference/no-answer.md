# No rows, a false premise, partial coverage, or data that cannot answer

- **No rows.** Ask Michael (answer_from_data, his second and last call) to confirm the filter matches values that exist in the data. When it still returns nothing, that is the answer: close from his reply.
- **The premise is false** (the user says X went up and it went down). Brief Michael to answer with what the data shows; do not answer the question as asked.
- **The data covers only part of the question** (the user asks about every World Cup and the data holds two; about a whole tournament and the data holds its last eight matches). Have Michael answer that part, with a brief that names what the data covers ("the 2018 and 2022 World Cups only") and asks him to say so before the figures.
- **The datasets cannot answer any of it** (a measure, a period or an entity they do not hold at all). Do not call Michael again.

**Reply:** say plainly what the data shows against what the question assumed. For partial coverage, the answer for the part covered, then what is missing to answer the whole question. When nothing can be answered, what is missing from the datasets and what would answer it.
